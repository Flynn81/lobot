---
name: devils-advocate
description: Stress-test a plan, argument, decision, forecast, or recommendation by finding the strongest counterarguments, hidden assumptions, missing alternatives, and realistic failure modes.
argument-hint: [plan or conclusion]
---

Stress-test the following:

$ARGUMENTS

Do not disagree for sport. Find the issues most likely to change the outcome.

Evaluate:
- assumptions that lack evidence;
- neglected alternatives;
- contrary evidence;
- base rates and historical precedent where relevant;
- operational and execution risk;
- incentives and stakeholder behavior;
- dependencies and single points of failure;
- downside severity and reversibility;
- what would falsify the thesis.

Return the 3-7 most important challenges, then propose modifications that would make the plan more robust.
