name: meeting-prep
description: Build a concise meeting briefing from available notes, documents, correspondence, calendar context, and external research. Use before an important meeting, interview, review, negotiation, or stakeholder conversation.
argument-hint: [meeting, attendees, or objective]
---

Prepare me for this meeting:

$ARGUMENTS

Use available context and connected information where permitted. Research external facts when relevant.

Produce:
- Meeting objective
- Attendees and why they matter
- Relevant prior context / commitments
- Current state and open issues
- Likely interests, concerns, or objections
- 3-7 questions I should ask
- Decisions or outcomes I should seek
- Risks / topics to avoid or handle carefully
- A 60-second pre-meeting briefing

Do not invent attendee motives; label inference as inference.
