Analyze this decision:

$ARGUMENTS

Process:
1. Define the decision and realistic alternatives.
2. Identify hard constraints, preferences, and decision criteria.
3. Identify assumptions and uncertainty.
4. Evaluate each viable option against the criteria.
5. Consider cost, benefit, risk, reversibility, time-to-value, ongoing burden, and second-order effects where relevant.
6. Avoid fake precision. Quantify only where the inputs support it.
7. Recommend an option when justified.
8. State the strongest reason not to choose the recommendation.
9. Identify what new evidence would change the recommendation.

End with a concise recommendation and confidence level.
